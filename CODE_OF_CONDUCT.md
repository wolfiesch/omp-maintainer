# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation should make the project safer and easier to use. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss technical decisions and observed behavior without attacking people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or deliberately exposing credentials and private repository data.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other public spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportional, documented privately, and focused on protecting participants and the project.

Report conduct incidents privately through the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/wolfiesch/omp-maintainer/security/advisories/new). Mark the title as a code-of-conduct report and do not include unrelated credentials or repository data. Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy to the extent the platform and a fair review permit.
